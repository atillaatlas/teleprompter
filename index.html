Paste your piece below. Camera mode scrolls it over the front camera and records; scroll mode is text only, for the laptop propped behind a phone. Everything stays on this device.

The script band sits only on your preview; the recording always shows your full face. Cam opens the lens list (Back 1, Back 2, Front...), ▤ resizes and lightens the band, and when the active lens can zoom you get preset steps plus a slider (leftmost is the widest view). Tapping the text mid-take pauses the recording and the scroll together; tap again and the same take continues. Keys on a laptop: space pause, arrows speed, Esc exit.
