Teleprompter

Paste your piece below. Camera mode scrolls it over the front camera and records; scroll mode is text only, for the laptop propped behind a phone. Everything stays on this device.

The script band sits only on your preview; the recording always shows your full face. Cam opens the lens list (Back 1, Back 2, Front...), ▤ resizes and lightens the band, and when the active lens can zoom you get preset steps plus a slider, and every lens opens at its widest. Fit switches the preview between the whole recorded frame and filling the screen; the recording always captures the whole frame. 180 turns the text and buttons upside down for flipped propping while your face stays upright. Hide tucks every control away for a clean view; the faint dot in the bottom corner brings them back. Fx cycles color looks (Warm, Soft, Film, B&W) that are baked into the recording; pick before you record. Tapping the text mid-take pauses the recording and the scroll together; tap again and the same take continues. Keys on a laptop: space pause, arrows speed, Esc exit.
